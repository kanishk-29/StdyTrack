Developer of Study Tracker

I'm Kanishk Kumar, a software developer. I build free, privacy-friendly web apps — and Study Tracker is the main one.

Study Tracker started from a simple irritation: most study apps either cost money, require an account, or scatter your notes, time logs, and exam scores across different screens. So I built one place to hold all of it — folders, subjects, units, lectures, rich-text notes, a study timer, exam scores, habits, and progress graphs. It installs as a PWA, works offline, and keeps your data in your own browser unless you choose to sync it.

What Study Tracker does

Organize: folders → subjects → units → lectures, with notes attached to each lecture.

Track time: a per-lecture timer plus a global study timer, logged automatically per subject and day.

Measure: exam scores, study streaks, topic coverage, and progress graphs built from your real data.

Own your data: everything is local-first; export and restore are built in.

It is free, there is no premium tier, and you do not need an account to try it. Open Study Tracker.
