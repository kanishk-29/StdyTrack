Study Tracker: The Study Tracker App That Organizes Your Notes, Time & Exam Scores

Scattered notes, forgotten deadlines, no idea where your time goes? Study Tracker is a free study tracker app built to fix exactly that — one place for every subject, every note, every hour, and every score.

Updated September 2026 · 7 min read

The Problem: Every Student Knows This Feeling

Have you ever found yourself the night before an exam, frantically flipping through a notebook, a phone note, a PDF, and a Google Doc — because your notes are scattered in five different places? Or sat down to "study" and realized two hours later you’d done almost nothing?

That’s not a lack of intelligence. It’s a lack of organization. When your study life is scattered across five apps, every extra minute spent looking for a note is a minute not spent learning. If you've searched for a study tracker app, a study planner, or a way to organize your study schedule, you've probably run into the same problems every time:

  • Notes everywhere. Handwritten, typed, screenshots, voice memos — nothing in one place, nothing searchable.
  • No time awareness. You have no idea how many hours actually went into each subject. Easy subjects get over-studied; hard ones get avoided.
  • Forgotten deadlines. Exams creep up because nothing is recorded or prioritized.
  • No feedback. You can't see progress. Without data, you can't tell if your study method is working.
  • Lost focus. Without a clear plan and structure, distractions win.

The Solution: One Study Tracker App for Everything

Study Tracker gives every student a single, organized home for their studies — so you spend your time learning, not searching. Here's how this study tracker app solves each part of the problem:

1. Organize Subjects, Units & Lectures

Structure your work the way your brain does: subject → unit → lecture. Everything lives in a clear hierarchy, so you always know where a note belongs and what you've covered.

2. Rich-Text Notes for Every Lecture

Take notes directly on each lecture — with bold, lists, headings, highlights, colors, and even drawings. Use multiple pages per note and search across all of them at once. No more flipping between a notebook and a phone.

3. A Study Timer That Shows Where Your Time Goes

Log how long you actually study each subject. See a breakdown of your hours so you can catch subjects you've been quietly avoiding and balance your week. Tracking your study time is the first step to controlling it.

4. Track Exam Scores & See Progress

Record your test scores and watch your performance improve subject by subject. Seeing progress on a graph is motivating — and it shows you what's actually working.

5. A Daily View to Stay on Track

Start each day with a clear view of what to study, what's due, and where your focus should be. Less mental noise, more momentum.

Why It Works

We're not here to tell you studying is easy. But organization removes the friction that makes studying harder than it needs to be. When your notes are in the right place, your time is tracked, and your plan is visible, the hard part becomes the learning — not the chaos around it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a study tracker app?

A study tracker app is a tool that organizes your subjects, lecture notes, study time, and exam scores in one place, so you can see your progress instead of guessing at it.

How do I stay organized while studying for multiple subjects?

Break each subject into units and lectures, keep notes for each lecture in one searchable place, log how long you actually study each subject, and review a daily plan instead of deciding what to study from scratch every day.

Is Study Tracker free to use?

Yes. Study Tracker runs free in your browser with no signup required. Your data is saved on your own device, with optional cloud sync if you want it available on more than one device.

What is the best way to track study hours?

Use a timer tied to each subject or lecture rather than estimating at the end of the week. Logged, per-subject time is what reveals which subjects you're avoiding before it becomes a problem near exams.
